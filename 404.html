About

I'm a full stack engineer who specializes in scalable React applications, data visualization, and modern product engineering.

I started out with a B.A. in Music before retraining as an engineer through Hack Reactor's immersive program in 2016 — which left me with an unusual amount of patience for iterating on details until they feel right. Since then I've worked across startups and enterprise environments, from building an e-commerce platform and partner APIs at ForeverCar, to contributing to JPMorgan Chase's internal design system, to running my own consultancy delivering full-stack products for clients like Carvana and Q2 Banking.

Today I'm a Senior UI Software Engineer at Publicis Media, where I lead frontend development on a React + TypeScript media analytics platform and build tooling that integrates AI-assisted workflows into everyday engineering. Outside of work, I've been applying that same AI-assisted approach to a personal project: designing real-time audio DSP plugins in C++ and Swift.
